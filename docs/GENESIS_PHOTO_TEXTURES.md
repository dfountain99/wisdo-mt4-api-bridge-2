# Genesis Photographic Surface Textures

Version: Genesis texture repair and world-surface upgrade, November 2026.

All maps are **Creative Commons CC0** photos or photobased surfaces, copied
from the RCForge MIT project's public scenery assets (the embedded bitmap
maps retain their upstream CC0 license). They are kept unchanged and shipped
as base64-encoded JPEG bytes for safe text-only repository content operations.

See the [RCForge upstream asset manifest](https://github.com/adithya-s-k/RCForge/blob/main/public/scenery/README.md).
Textures are only used for in-game visuals; they do not change gameplay, saved
biome coordinates, collision, mining yields, or security/trading controls.
This is a significant photographic surface upgrade, **not** a claim of completed
photorealistic creature models, physically measured surfaces, or guaranteed FPS.
Browser rendering, low-memory Safari behavior, and remaining low-poly props need
on-device validation.
